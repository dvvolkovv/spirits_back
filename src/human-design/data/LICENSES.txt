Third-party software and data used by the Human Design calculator
https://linkeon.io/calculators/human-design/

The chart is calculated in the browser with Astronomy Engine. The list of
cities and time zones comes from GeoNames, country names in the
non-Russian lists from Unicode CLDR, simplified Chinese characters from
Unicode Unihan. Their licenses follow.

2. GeoNames
   https://www.geonames.org/

Cities with a population over 15,000 and capitals (cities15000), country
and region names (countryInfo, admin1CodesASCII), their names in the site
languages — Russian, English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese
(alternateNamesV2) and IANA time zones, from the dump of 14 October 2026.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: only the name (in the file's language and in Latin), country,
region, time zone and population (rounded to thousands) are kept;
Europe/Kyiv is written as Europe/Kiev for older browsers. In the
non-Russian files country names come from Unicode CLDR (see 3), Chinese
names are converted to simplified characters with Unihan (see 3), source
artefacts such as "A or B", a trailing apostrophe and, in Chinese, a
trailing country or state ("，…", "（…）") are removed. The names of some
places were corrected by hand (scripts/hd-city-overrides.tsv).
The files (one per language) are built by scripts/build-hd-cities.py
(https://github.com/dvvolkovv/land_linkeon).


3. Unicode CLDR and Unihan
   https://cldr.unicode.org/
   https://www.unicode.org/charts/unihan.html

come from the Unicode Common Locale Data Repository (CLDR 48.0, as shipped
with ICU 78.2 in Node.js 22). Traditional-to-simplified character variants
for Chinese names (kSimplifiedVariant) come from the Unicode Han Database
(Unihan, Unicode 18.0.0, Unihan_Variants.txt of 31 July 2026). Licensed
